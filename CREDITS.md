# Credits

## Character models

The people in the game are built from [MakeHuman](http://www.makehumancommunity.org) data by
`tools/blender/build_humans.py`: the base mesh, its shape targets, rig weights, skins, eyes,
eyebrows, eyelashes, hair, shoes and the casual suits and work suit are MakeHuman system
assets, released as **CC0** by Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier.
